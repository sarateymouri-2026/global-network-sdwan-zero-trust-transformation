# Architecture Principles

## Business Alignment
Technology investments must support business goals.

## Security by Design
Security controls must be integrated into all architecture domains.

## Cloud First
Cloud services are strategic platforms for business agility and scalability.

## Zero Trust
Access must be continuously validated based on identity and context.

## Standardization
Global standards should be applied across all regions.

## Resilience
Systems should be designed for high availability and business continuity.

## Operational Simplicity
Reduce complexity and improve visibility through automation and governance.

## AI-Augmented Operations
Leverage AI capabilities to improve monitoring, analytics and operational efficiency.
