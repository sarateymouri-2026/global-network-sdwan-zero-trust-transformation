# Business Drivers

## Purpose

The purpose of this transformation program is to align SATECH Manufacturing's network, cloud and security capabilities with evolving business requirements, operational objectives and long-term growth initiatives.

As the organization expands globally and increases its dependence on cloud-based services, the existing network and security operating model is becoming increasingly complex, costly and difficult to scale.

The transformation program aims to establish a modern, secure and globally standardized foundation capable of supporting future business demands.
