# Target State Architecture

## Purpose

The target-state architecture defines the future operating model for network, cloud, security and operational services across SATECH Manufacturing.

The architecture is designed to support global growth, cloud adoption, operational resilience, cybersecurity modernization and AI-enabled operations through a unified transformation strategy.

The objective is to create a scalable, secure and intelligent technology platform capable of supporting 40,000 users across 160 sites in 40 countries.

---

# Strategic Vision

SATECH Manufacturing will transform from a traditional MPLS-centric and perimeter-based environment into a cloud-connected, identity-driven and AI-enabled enterprise architecture.

The future-state platform will provide:

- Global connectivity standardization
- Cloud-first service delivery
- Zero Trust security
- AI-assisted operations
- Simplified governance
- Enhanced business agility
- Improved user experience

The architecture will be built around five strategic domains:

1. Network & Connectivity
2. Cloud & Hybrid Infrastructure
3. Security & Zero Trust
4. AI-Driven Operations
5. Governance & Service Management

---

# Architecture Principles

## Business Alignment

Technology investments must directly support business strategy, operational objectives and future growth.

## Cloud First

Cloud services are strategic platforms and connectivity must be optimized for secure access to business applications regardless of location.

## Security by Design

Security controls must be integrated into every architecture domain and enforced consistently.

## Identity-Centric Access

Identity becomes the new security perimeter.

## Standardization

Global standards and operating procedures must be applied whenever possible.

## Operational Simplicity

Reduce complexity, improve visibility and increase automation capabilities.

## Resilience

Eliminate single points of failure and improve business continuity capabilities.

## AI-Augmented Operations

Leverage AI technologies to improve operational efficiency, visibility and decision-making.
